FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about our pet boarding services — how we care for your beloved pets, and what to expect before your first stay.

Still have a question?
What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve pet families throughout El Dorado County, including Placerville, Diamond Springs, Shingle Springs, El Dorado Hills, Pollock Pines, Camino, and surrounding communities. Many clients travel from neighboring areas because they trust our personalized care approach and kennel-free environment that keeps their pets happy and comfortable.

Do you require vaccinations for dog boarding and daycare services?

Yes, we require current vaccination records for all pets to ensure the health and safety of every animal in our care. Please bring vaccination documentation during your initial consultation so we can review requirements and ensure your dog meets all health standards for boarding or daycare services.

Can you accommodate pets with special dietary or medical needs?

Absolutely! Our experienced team regularly cares for pets with special dietary requirements, medication schedules, and medical needs. We work closely with pet owners to understand specific care instructions and maintain detailed records to ensure consistent, proper care throughout their stay at our Placerville facility.

What should I bring when boarding my dog or cat?

Please bring your pet's regular food, any medications with clear instructions, vaccination records, and detailed care preferences. Having familiar items like their favorite toy, blanket, or crate helps pets feel more comfortable and secure during their stay away from home.

Do you offer facility tours before booking services?

Yes, we encourage all pet owners to visit our facility and meet our team before booking services. Tours help you see our kennel-free environment, outdoor play areas, and specialized spaces while discussing your pet's specific needs with our experienced, caring staff.

What types of exotic pets do you care for?

We welcome birds, rabbits, reptiles, and other small exotic pets, providing specialized care appropriate for each species. Our team has experience with various animals and can accommodate most pets with proper advance notice. Please contact us to discuss your exotic pet's specific requirements.

How do you handle emergencies during pet boarding?

We have established relationships with local Placerville veterinary clinics and maintain emergency protocols for any health concerns. If issues arise, we immediately contact you and seek appropriate veterinary care. Your pet's safety and health are always our top priorities, day or night.
